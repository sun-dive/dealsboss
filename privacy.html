Privacy

Last updated 16 September 2026. This page describes what actually happens, in plain words. Where a fact changes, this page changes with it.

There is no account

This site has nothing to sign up for. It asks for no name, email address or password, and it has no way to identify you.

What this site keeps

  • Server logs. The site is on shared hosting, whose web server keeps the standard log of requests: address, time, page asked for. The hosting company keeps these on its own schedule. We do not analyse them and we do not add to them.
  • Contact messages, if you send one: the message and whatever you chose to write in the reply line, delivered to our mailbox and kept until dealt with.
  • Nothing else.

Cookies

This site sets no cookies and loads nothing from anyone else: no analytics, no social buttons, no advertising code.

Promotions in our apps

A promotion shown inside one of our apps is an image we host and a link we chose. Showing it tells us only that it was shown, and tapping it tells us only that it was tapped, as counts with nothing attached. Nothing about you is recorded, and nothing from the merchant loads inside the app.

When you follow a link

A promotion leads to the merchant's own site. From that moment you are on their site under their privacy policy, and they may set their own cookies, as any site does. Some links carry a code that tells the merchant the visit came from Deals Boss, so that we are paid a commission if you buy. The code identifies us, not you.

Children

This site collects nothing from anyone and has no features aimed at children.

Changes

If what the site does changes, this page is updated and the date at the top moves.
